## ⟡ About

> *AI/ML engineer with a software-first mindset - I don't just train models, I ship them.*

I'm an **AI/ML graduate** completing a **Master of Applied Artificial Intelligence (Professional)** at **Deakin University** (expected Nov 2026), building on a Bachelor's in Electronics, Communication and Information Engineering. My work spans the full AI solution lifecycle - prototyping and experimenting with AI/automation tools, preparing and exploring data for model development, and monitoring and testing AI-generated outputs for quality, accuracy, and responsible use.

My current postgraduate research investigates **clean-label backdoor attacks in CNNs**, which has sharpened my ability to identify AI risk, apply structured evidence-based evaluation, and design for responsible AI in production systems. On the engineering side, I build **RAG pipelines**, **agentic AI systems** (OpenAI Agents SDK, Google ADK), and traditional ML solutions (regression, random forests, XGBoost) - always translating ambiguous business problems into practical, deployable AI.

I'm curious, resilient, and comfortable leading team-based technical projects end-to-end - from planning through delivery - while building strong relationships across engineering, data, and stakeholder teams.
